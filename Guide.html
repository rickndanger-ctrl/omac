Agent Control Center

Open the app, then use ▦ Control in the menu bar. Enter creates or reuses your four agent terminals and arranges ChatGPT, Claude, and Chrome in Research.

Control–Option + arrowsFocus a tile
Control–Option–Shift + arrowsMove a tile
Control–Option + 1 / 2Agents / Research
Control–Option + minus / equalsResize
Control–Option + FEnlarge / restore tile
Control–Option + SpaceFloat / tile focused window
Control–Option + PPause tiling and shortcuts
Control–Option + EscapeExit and restore windows

Resume from the menu bar. Exit does not close your terminals or agents. Trackpad pointing, clicking, and scrolling work normally; custom gestures are not installed.

First use: grant Agent Control Center and AeroSpace Accessibility access in System Settings. This is required to move windows. No login startup is installed.

The Local tile uses your existing Qwen launcher and requires your existing Full Local mode. It does not change model selections. Agents open normally; no prompt is automatically submitted.

Closing a terminal ends its foreground session. Exit Control Center preserves open sessions but does not provide recovery across terminal closure or reboot. Window restoration is best effort for windows still matching their original app, position in its window list, and title.

Emergency fallback: quit AeroSpace from its menu bar. Rollback instructions are in README.md alongside this guide.
